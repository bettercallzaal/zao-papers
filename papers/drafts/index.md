## How to Contribute to Drafts

1. **You have an idea:** Write it as a draft in `drafts/<name>.md` and open a PR.
2. **Comment on a draft:** Reply on the PR with feedback, concerns, or corrections.
3. **Move to published:** Once a draft receives community feedback and is ready for ratification or publication, it moves out of `drafts/` into the parent directory and changes status from "Draft" to "Review" or "Accepted".

## Guidelines for Draft Authors

- Ground everything in verified sources (Doc NNN, code, on-chain data)
- Mark uncertain claims with [to confirm]
- Use the same style guide as published papers (no emojis, exact brand spellings)
- Include open questions and unknowns - that is the point of a draft
- Cite related ZIPs and research documents
