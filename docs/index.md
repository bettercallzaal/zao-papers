# The ZAO Papers

Welcome to The ZAO's canonical home for governance documents, research papers, and ecosystem documentation.

## What You'll Find Here

**The ZAO Papers** is where The ZAO community proposes, discusses, and ratifies governance changes. We publish:

- **Governance ZIPs** - Formal proposals for changes to The Fractal, Respect distribution, treasury policy, and organizational structure
- **Research Papers** - Deep dives into ecosystem projects like Sparkz, brand documentation, and architectural proposals
- **Drafts** - Works in progress that are gathering community feedback

## The ZAO Ecosystem

The ZAO is a Respect-weighted governance network coordinating music, culture, and community projects. Our brands include:

- **WaveWarZ** - Artist-to-fan music trading game with real revenue splits
- **ZABAL Games** - 3-month build-a-thon with mentorship from industry partners
- **Sparkz** - Configurable creator-coin launcher with energy-first coordination
- **COC Concertz** - Emerging artist concert series and record label
- **ZAO Festivals** - IRL event programming (ZAO-STOCK, ZAOCHELLA, and more)

## How to Contribute

1. **Comment on papers** - Use the "💬 Comment / Collaborate on GitHub" link at the bottom of any paper
2. **Propose a ZIP** - New governance ideas go through formal review (see [ZIP Process](governance/process.md))
3. **Submit research** - Have a paper on an ecosystem project? Open a PR with your draft
