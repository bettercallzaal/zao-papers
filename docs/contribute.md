# Contribute

The ZAO Papers is an open, community-reviewed body of work. Anyone can propose a paper, a governance ZIP, or a correction. Here is how.

## Propose a paper or draft

1. **Write it.** Add your document as `papers/<name>.md` (a research paper) or `papers/drafts/<name>.md` (a work-in-progress gathering feedback). Keep it focused, cite your sources, and mark anything unverified as `[to confirm]`.
2. **Open a pull request** against this repo. The paper renders on the site automatically once merged; drafts land under the Drafts section.
3. **Gather feedback.** Every published page has a "Comment / Collaborate on GitHub" link at the bottom that opens a GitHub Discussion for that page. Reviewers can also comment directly on your PR.
4. **Move to published.** Once a draft has been reviewed and is ready, it moves out of `papers/drafts/` into `papers/` and its status changes from Draft to Review or Accepted.

## Propose a governance change (ZIP)

Governance changes go through the formal ZAO Improvement Proposal process:

1. Draft your proposal from the [ZIP template](https://github.com/bettercallzaal/zao-papers/blob/main/zips/zip-template.md) as `zips/zip-NNNN-title.md` (use `NNNN` = TBD if you do not have a number yet).
2. Open a PR. The repo owner assigns the ZIP number.
3. Review happens in GitHub Discussions and The ZAO Discord, then in a weekly Fractal session, then on-chain via ORDAO/OREC when the change is blockchain-relevant.

See the [ZIP Process](governance/process.md) for the full lifecycle and status definitions.

## Correct or improve an existing page

Every page has an edit (pencil) link in the top right that opens the source file on GitHub. Small fixes - typos, broken links, stale facts - are welcome as direct PRs. For substantive changes to an Accepted ZIP, open a new ZIP that references and amends the original rather than rewriting it in place.

## Ground rules

- **Accuracy over completeness.** Cite research docs, on-chain data, or code for every technical claim. Flag gaps with `[to confirm]` rather than guessing.
- **No emojis, no em dashes.** Use hyphens. Match the spartan house voice.
- **Brand spellings are exact:** The ZAO, WaveWarZ, ZABAL Games, Sparkz, COC Concertz, ORDAO, OREC, Respect, Fractal, Farcaster.
