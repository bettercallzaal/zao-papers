## How to Read These Profiles

- **Grounded facts** appear as plain text with full names, roles, and documented contributions.
- **Open questions** appear as `[to confirm with Zaal]` blocks. These are intentional gaps, not forgotten details.
- **Context quality** is tagged as rich, moderate, or thin. This is transparency about what we know and what we are guessing about.
- **No invention.** If a date, title, location, or relationship is not documented, it is marked as open or omitted entirely.

## Submission & Review

New member profiles can be submitted as PRs to this repository. The process:

1. **Research.** Ground the profile in verified sources - memory docs, research files, on-chain data, public handles, shipped work.
2. **Draft.** Write using the template below. Mark open questions as `[to confirm]`.
3. **PR.** Submit the PR with status: Draft. Request review from @zaal.
4. **Feedback.** Zaal provides context for the open questions or corrects any inaccuracies.
5. **Revision.** Update the profile. When reviewed, change status to Accepted.

## Style

- No emojis or decorative Unicode.
- No em dashes (use hyphens).
- Prose, not bullet lists.
- Brand spellings exact (The ZAO, not "ZAO"; WaveWarZ, not "Wave Wars").
- Honest about what we do not know.

## Purpose

These profiles are part of The ZAO's institutional memory. They celebrate the people who build this ecosystem, ground the community in real humans (not abstractions), and create a record of who shaped what and why. They are living documents - as people contribute more, their profiles deepen.

The goal is simple: when someone new to The ZAO asks "who is candytoybox?" or "what did JANGOUU FOREVER do?", they find a grounded, honest answer.
