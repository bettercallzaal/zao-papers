# candytoybox - Design First

Samantha, known as candytoybox across The ZAO ecosystem, is one of the founding designers and strategic partners of WaveWarZ, the first project to graduate from The ZAO incubator model. She brings design discipline and marketing sensibility to everything she touches - the visual identity of WaveWarZ, the promo strategy, the community-facing experience.

## WaveWarZ: The Cofounder Story

WaveWarZ was the proof that The ZAO incubator model could work. Hurric4n3ike founded it with the technical vision - a music-battle platform where artists compete head-to-head and the community participates in the outcomes. Zaal backed it as an incubator. Samantha (candytoybox) joined as a cofounder, bringing the design and community voice that transformed it from a technical idea into something with real user momentum. The three together - technical founder, visionary incubator, and design strategist - became the template for how The ZAO incubates new projects.

WaveWarZ has run hundreds of battles across Solana and Base. It hosted a live IRL battle at a ZAO event, proving the concept works in physical space too. That convergence of on-chain and real-world is the kind of boundary dissolution Samantha seems drawn to.

## Design + Marketing = Her Signature

Within The ZAO orbit, Samantha is the design person. Not just visual design - the full integration of how a project looks, feels, moves, and speaks to creators. She leads the design for WaveWarZ and extends that vision across ZABAL Games, the events, the brand architecture. Promo and marketing flow from the same place: if the design is right, the story tells itself.

This is what "deff design" means in The ZAO lexicon - thorough, grounded, not decorative. Candy designs so the product works and the creator feels the difference.
