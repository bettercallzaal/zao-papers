# JANGOUU FOREVER - The Origin

JANGOUU FOREVER (all caps, both words - that is the canonical spelling) is the musician at the heart of The ZAO's origin story. Working with him is what shifted Zaal from building tools to thinking entrepreneurially about how to support independent artists at scale.

## The Story

In college, around 2018 or 2019, Zaal met a talented independent musician and watched what happened next. The musician had real skill, real output, real audience potential. But the systems available to independent artists - distribution deals, licensing splits, data access, IP rights - took the margin, the data, and the rights away. The profit went everywhere except to the artist who made the work.

That experience, working closely with JANGOUU, planted the seed. It grew into the question: what if an artist could keep the profit margin, the data, and the IP rights? What if the infrastructure was built by artists, for artists, instead of by intermediaries extracting value?

That question became The ZAO.
